# VitalBranch / Integrative Medicine Startup — Startup Due Diligence Pack

Prepared 2026-08-20. Subject: proposed startup combining Western medicine + TCM + Ayurveda + Jamu
into one decision-support / recommendation layer.  
Mock-up reviewed:
- `https://miracle-injuly.pplx.app`
- `https://miracle-in19aug.base44.app/`

## How to use this pack

- Read [00-executive-summary](`00-executive-summary.md`) first. It contains the verdict and the five decisions that
  determine whether this is a company or a hobby.
- [01-mockup-teardown](`01-mockup-teardown.md`) is the evidence base — everything in it was observed directly in the
  live mock-up on 2026-08-20 and is reproducible. Use it as the shared factual ground with your
  co-founder so the conversation is about artefacts, not opinions.
- [pivot-options](`08-pivot-options.md`) + [90day-validation-plan](`09-90-day-validation-plan.md`) are the constructive half. Bring these,
  not just the risk list, or the conversation becomes "engineer says no".
- [cofounder-terms-and-risks](`10-cofounder-terms-and-risks.md`) is about you, not the product. Do not skip it.

## One-line summary

The problem is real and under-served; the current product framing is the least fundable and most
liability-exposed version of it. There is a narrower business here — **safety, provenance and
distribution infrastructure for integrative practice** — that is fundable, defensible, and can be
validated in 90 days for under USD 25k. See [pivot options](`08-pivot-options.md`).
